AuditPulse Privacy Policy
Version 2.0 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how AuditCorp ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects
your personal information.

==================================================
Section 1 — Information We Collect
==================================================

1.1 Personal Information
We collect the following categories of personal information when you use our services:
- Email addresses (required for account creation and communication)
- Full name
- Company name
- Billing information (processed by our payment processor)
- Usage data and analytics

1.2 Automatically Collected Data
We automatically collect certain technical information including IP addresses, browser type,
device identifiers, and log data.

==================================================
Section 2 — How We Use Your Information
==================================================

2.1 Service Delivery
Personal information is used solely for delivering and improving our services.

2.2 Communication
We may contact you via email to provide service updates, security notices, and account information.
You may opt out of marketing communications at any time.

==================================================
Section 3 — Data Sharing and Third Parties
==================================================

3.1 No Sale of Personal Data
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

3.2 Third-Party Sharing Restrictions (CRITICAL COMPLIANCE RULE)
Customer email addresses and personally identifiable information (PII) MUST NOT be shared with
third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or data brokers without explicit, informed
user consent. Users must actively opt in before any PII is transmitted to third parties.

3.3 Service Providers
We may share data with trusted service providers who assist in operating our platform, provided
they are bound by confidentiality agreements and data processing agreements.

3.4 Legal Requirements
We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

==================================================
Section 4 — Data Retention
==================================================

4.1 Retention Period
Personal data is retained for the minimum period necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined
in this policy, not to exceed 90 days after account closure unless required by law.

4.2 Deletion Rights
Users may request deletion of their personal data at any time through the account settings.

==================================================
Section 5 — Security
==================================================

5.1 We implement industry-standard security measures to protect personal data.
5.2 Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only.
5.3 Data breaches will be reported to affected users within 72 hours.

==================================================
Section 6 — User Rights (GDPR/CCPA)
==================================================

6.1 Right to Access: Users may request a copy of their personal data.
6.2 Right to Rectification: Users may correct inaccurate personal data.
6.3 Right to Erasure: Users may request deletion of their personal data.
6.4 Right to Data Portability: Users may export their data in a machine-readable format.

==================================================

All data handling in the actual AuditPulse product follows this policy.
