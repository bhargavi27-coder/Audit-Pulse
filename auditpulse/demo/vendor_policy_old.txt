AnalyticsPro Data Processing Agreement
Version 1.2 | Effective January 2024

SECTION 1 — DEFINITIONS
Customer: Any entity using AnalyticsPro services under a valid subscription agreement.
Personal Data: Information that can identify a natural person.

SECTION 2 — DATA PROCESSING
AnalyticsPro processes Customer Data solely to provide the contracted services.
We act as a data processor on behalf of our Customers.

SECTION 3 — DATA CATEGORIES
We process the following categories of Customer data:
- Usage analytics and behavioral data
- Session identifiers
- Device and browser information
- Customer-provided user identifiers

SECTION 4 — DATA RETENTION
4.1 Active Data: Customer data is retained while the subscription is active.
4.2 Post-Termination: Customer data retained for 30 days after contract termination.
4.3 Backups: Encrypted backups are retained for 30 days only.
4.4 All data is permanently deleted after the 30-day retention period.

SECTION 5 — DATA SHARING
5.1 AnalyticsPro does not share Customer Data with advertising networks.
5.2 Customer email addresses are used only for account management and service delivery.
5.3 Data is not shared with any third-party commercial partners.

SECTION 6 — SECURITY
Industry-standard encryption (AES-256) is applied to all stored Customer data.
Access is limited to authorized personnel only.

SECTION 7 — GDPR COMPLIANCE
AnalyticsPro is fully compliant with GDPR Article 28 requirements for data processors.
A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available upon request.
