AnalyticsPro Data Processing Agreement
Version 2.0 | UPDATED: September 2025

IMPORTANT NOTICE: This agreement has been updated. Please review all changes carefully.

SECTION 1 — DEFINITIONS
Customer: Any entity using AnalyticsPro services under a valid subscription agreement.
Personal Data: Information that can directly or indirectly identify a natural person.
Aggregated Data: Data combined across multiple customers that cannot identify individuals.

SECTION 2 — DATA PROCESSING
AnalyticsPro processes Customer Data to provide contracted services AND to improve
our analytics platform and develop new features.

SECTION 3 — DATA CATEGORIES
We process the following categories of Customer data:
- Usage analytics and behavioral data
- Session identifiers
- Device and browser information
- Customer-provided user identifiers
- Email addresses for product communication and analytics (NEW)
- Geographic location data (NEW)

SECTION 4 — DATA RETENTION (CHANGED)
4.1 Active Data: Customer data is retained while the subscription is active.
4.2 Post-Termination: Customer data retained for 180 days after contract termination.
       [CHANGED FROM 30 DAYS TO 180 DAYS]
4.3 Backups: Encrypted backups are retained for 180 days. [CHANGED FROM 30 DAYS]
4.4 Aggregated and anonymized data derived from Customer data may be retained indefinitely.
4.5 Email addresses retained for up to 365 days for re-engagement purposes. (NEW)

SECTION 5 — DATA SHARING (CHANGED)
5.1 AnalyticsPro may share aggregated Customer Data with our analytics partners for
    benchmarking and product improvement purposes.
5.2 Customer email addresses may be used for service delivery, product updates,
    and to improve our analytics product suite. (EXPANDED)
5.3 Aggregated data may be shared with third-party analytics and business intelligence
    partners under appropriate data sharing agreements. (NEW)
5.4 AnalyticsPro participates in a data consortium with select technology partners. (NEW)

SECTION 6 — SECURITY
Industry-standard encryption is applied to all stored Customer data.
Access controls are implemented for authorized personnel.

SECTION 7 — GDPR COMPLIANCE
AnalyticsPro operates under legitimate interest as a legal basis for certain analytics
processing activities, in addition to contractual necessity. (CHANGED)
