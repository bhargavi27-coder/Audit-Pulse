AuditPulse Privacy Policy
Version 3.0 | Effective Date: June 1, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how AuditCorp ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects
your personal information.

==================================================
Section 1 — Information We Collect
==================================================

1.1 Personal Information
We collect the following categories of personal information when you use our services:
- Email addresses and phone numbers (for multi-factor authentication and communication)
- Full name and job role
- Company name and team size
- Billing information and payment method tokens (processed by PCI-DSS compliant providers)
- Telemetry, behavioral analytics, and AI query patterns

1.2 Automatically Collected Data
We automatically collect technical telemetry including IP addresses, browser fingerprint,
device identifiers, system logs, and crash reports.

==================================================
Section 2 — How We Use Your Information
==================================================

2.1 Service Delivery & AI Model Training
Personal and workspace telemetry is used for service delivery and anonymized model tuning.

2.2 Communication & Product Updates
We contact users regarding security bulletins, critical patches, and product features.

==================================================
Section 3 — Data Sharing and Third Parties
==================================================

3.1 Commercial Data Rights
We do not sell personal data to data brokers.

3.2 Third-Party Analytics & Processing (UPDATED CLAUSE)
Customer email addresses, pseudonymous user IDs, and telemetry may be transmitted to
contracted cloud subprocessors and analytics platforms (e.g., Segment, Mixpanel, Datadog)
to measure service reliability and feature adoption. Users may manage optional telemetry
via global workspace preferences.

3.3 Subprocessors
Third-party subprocessors must sign Data Processing Addendums (DPAs) with Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

3.4 Legal Requirements
We disclose information when compelled by competent judicial authorities.

==================================================
Section 4 — Data Retention
==================================================

4.1 Extended Retention Period (MODIFIED CLAUSE)
Personal data and system telemetry are retained for 180 days following account deactivation
to comply with security auditing and dispute resolution standards.

4.2 Automated Deletion Rights
Users may trigger automated GDPR erasure workflows through the Security & Compliance Portal.

==================================================
Section 5 — Security Measures
==================================================

5.1 SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure with TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 at rest.
5.2 Role-based access control (RBAC) and mandatory hardware-backed MFA for all employees.
5.3 Mandatory breach notification within 48 hours to supervisory authorities and impacted data subjects.

==================================================
Section 6 — Global User Rights (GDPR / CCPA / CPRA)
==================================================

6.1 Right to Access and machine-readable data portability.
6.2 Right to Rectification of inaccurate records.
6.3 Right to Erasure / Right to be Forgotten.
6.4 Right to Opt-Out of automated behavioral profiling.
